## INSTRUCTIONS FOR CHATGPT

You are generating still frames for a short **observational documentary** about a rainy mountain county town in northeast Yunnan, China. Every image must look like a frame grab from real documentary footage shot on location. If an image looks like a movie still, a TV drama, an advertisement, a painting or AI art, it has failed.

1. Generate the items of the PART the user names, in order, ONE image per reply. "下一个" or "next" means the next item; "重做" or "redo" means regenerate the same item with the user's feedback.
2. For normal items, build the prompt as: STYLE + REGION + item prompt + PEOPLE (if people appear) + PALETTE & AVOID. For EDIT items, use only the edit text and keep everything not mentioned exactly as it is.
3. Landscape 3:2 (1536×1024), filled edge to edge. Never add borders, captions, watermarks, logos or readable text.
4. Start every reply with the item ID in bold and end with one line: the file name to save it as.
5. When in doubt, make the image plainer, flatter, softer and more ordinary. Never beautify.

### STYLE
```
Observational documentary still, a frame grab from a digital cinema camera shot on location. Only the light that is really there: overcast diffuse daylight, fog, a window, a bare bulb or a stove flame; no added lighting. Deep focus. Eye-level tripod unless stated otherwise. Not HDR, no over-sharpening, soft natural micro-contrast, slightly muted colors straight from the camera, neutral white balance, slightly underexposed, humid air. Slightly untidy and imperfect, like real life.
```

### REGION
```
Northeast Yunnan mountain county, about 1,200 m altitude: a small basin town ringed by steep green slopes with patchy dry-land fields of corn, potato and chili, some under plastic mulch film; bamboo groves; low clouds on the ridges. The town is 4–6 storey self-built concrete houses, some tiled white or beige, many bare concrete; blue or white corrugated metal awnings; stainless steel window grilles; air-conditioner units; tangled power lines; small shops with roll-up shutters; wet asphalt; e-bikes and motorcycles with full rain capes. Rural houses mix old timber-frame houses with grey clay tile roofs and newer flat-roofed concrete houses, with concrete courtyards, firewood stacks and woven plastic sacks.
```

### PEOPLE
```
Ordinary local residents in worn, faded, mismatched everyday clothes, going about their business, not looking at the camera, not posing, small in the frame or turned away. Real, uneven skin; no makeup; no ethnic costume.
```

### PALETTE & AVOID
```
Palette: grey-green mountains, wet cement grey, old white tiles, brownish brick, dull chili red only where the prompt asks for it, coal-flame blue. Avoid: cinematic lighting, rim light, light rays, shallow depth of field, bokeh, golden hour glow, lens flare, teal-and-orange grade, HDR, glossy or retouched skin, costumes or ethnic dress, tourist-town or old-town architecture, rice-paddy terraces, staged symmetry, red umbrellas or red clothing, readable text or logos. Keep the top and bottom 8% of the frame free of important detail.
```

## 常见问题与重做说法

| 问题 | 回复的说法 |
|---|---|
| 像电影、短剧或广告 | Make it look like an ordinary unedited frame from a documentary: flat, plain light, neutral colors, softer, nothing dramatic, nothing beautified. |
| 太锐、太艳（AI 摄影感） | Less sharp, less saturated, lower contrast, like an unprocessed camera frame. No HDR. |
| 背景虚化 | Deep focus: make the background as sharp as the foreground. No bokeh. |
| 改图时构图变了 | Keep the camera position and every object exactly where they are in the attached image. Change only what I asked for. |
| 人物像演员或看镜头 | Ordinary local residents, small in the frame or turned away, busy, not looking at the camera. |
| 出现文字或乱码 | Remove all text, letters, signs and logos. |
